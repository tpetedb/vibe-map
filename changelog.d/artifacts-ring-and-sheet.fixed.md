- The yellow ring around an artifact is the Inspect zone: it is drawn at the
  radius the walk-up test uses, so the fountain's ring is out on the grass
  instead of under the lake, the mountain's is clear of the mountain, and
  "walk up to the yellow ring" is true wherever you cross one.
- Pressing a second demo button cancels the lines the first one still owed,
  instead of interleaving two transcripts in the same terminal.
- Under reduced motion a demo prints its whole transcript at once, and the
  terminal is a polite live region, so a screen reader hears what came back.
- The "Do it for real" walkthrough is drawn without ligatures: a step that
  says to print `GET /coffee -> 200` exactly can be copied off the screen and
  still match the check, on a font that would draw the arrow as one glyph.
- The vault links at the foot of an artifact sheet are links: the vault's
  colour and underline, a focus ring, and Enter opens the note.
- The balloon demo charges the region it rents in: t3.small in `eu-west-1` is
  0.0228 USD an hour, and a month of forgetting it is 16.42 USD.
- The mountain demo names `vibemap/cli.py`, the file the package actually has.
