- The island pauses when you look away. Switching tab or locking the phone
  stops the clock the ambient animations run on, and coming back puts it down
  on the frame it left, so an hour in another app is not an hour of boats,
  birds and weather in one jump. Battery saver in Settings turns it off.
- A short buzz on a claim, on an achievement and on a collectible, where the
  browser has one (Android Chrome; iOS has no vibration at all). It is gated by
  the Buzz setting, stays silent under reduced motion, and is never asked for
  before you have touched the page, which is the only time the browser would
  take it.
- The Backpack has a Notifications tab: every message the island has shown you,
  newest first, with the time it said it. A toast still goes away after five
  seconds, but what it said is kept, so a repaired record, an achievement or a
  find that arrived while you were reading can be read late. Quiet mode now
  raises no toast at all and keeps the list, and at most three cards are on
  screen at once, because a record that earns four achievements together used
  to fill a phone with them.
- A first-time hint shows once and never again, and Settings already had the
  button that brings them back.
- A quiet "Saved" mark after the record is written, throttled, because save is
  called on every pickup, every claim and every setting.
