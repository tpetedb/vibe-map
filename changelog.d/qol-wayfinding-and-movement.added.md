- Walk me there. A stop you pick in the search palette (Cmd K, then the Walk
  there button or Shift and Enter), a plot you tap on the map, a row in the
  big map's list of stops: the walker sets off for it and the island says
  which one it is going to. One seam, `walkTo(n)`, is what every panel calls.
  The Roadmap has no Walk there button yet, and a stop on another island is
  not walked to: both come with the panels follow-up to this batch.
- A walk goes round what is in its way. The way is worked out when the
  destination is taken, round the hub, the lake and the bays, for a tap on
  the ground as much as for a stop. A walk that cannot get any nearer, to a
  tap on the hub's roof or across a shut bridge, ends there and says so
  instead of pressing against the wall under a lit marker, and leaving the
  island ends it too, by a bridge, by the World button, or by the cut that
  reduced motion makes of the flight.
- An arrow at the edge of the screen points at the next stop with the distance
  to it, and at the bridge that has opened once the island is finished. It
  says where to go and takes no press of its own, so a tap where it happens to
  be is a tap on the island under it. It is off at the two hardest
  difficulties. The mode is kept as `S.settings.guide` (on, next, off), but
  Settings has no row for it yet, so for now the difficulty is the only switch
  a player has; the row comes with the same follow-up.
- The marker where you are walking to stays lit until you arrive instead of
  fading after a second, with dashes on the ground from the walker to it that
  follow the way round. Each dash is a light plate on a dark one, so it stands
  out from grass, snow, sand and lava rock alike.
- The map is a map you can use: tap a plot to walk there, make it bigger with
  a button of its own (or a long press on a phone), and every stop is a
  labelled button under it for a keyboard or a screen reader.
- Hurrying: hold Shift, or push the on-screen stick to its rim, to move half
  again as fast. Settings already carried "Tap to hurry" for a hand that
  cannot hold a key down, and it now does what it says. Shift used with Tab
  does not toggle hurrying, and changing apps releases held movement keys.
- Battery saver: a minute with nothing touched and no panel open draws half
  the frames, and any key, tap or stick is full rate again at once. Settings
  carried the switch; this is the frame loop obeying it.
