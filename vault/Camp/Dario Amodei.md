# Dario Amodei

*CEO and co-founder, Anthropic*

Co-author of the 2020 scaling-laws paper, former VP of research at OpenAI, co-founded Anthropic in 2021 with a safety-first thesis. Wrote Core Views on AI Safety and the essay Machines of Loving Grace on what could go right.

**What they would tell you**
- Scaling is predictable; that is both the opportunity and the reason for caution.
- Interpretability and alignment research have to keep pace with capability.
- Be honest about uncertainty, in both directions.

**Going deeper**
The scaling-laws paper is where the power-law plots come from. Core Views explains why Anthropic trains frontier models at all while worrying about them. Machines of Loving Grace is the optimistic case, written to be argued with. Read both and you understand the company behind the tool you are using.
