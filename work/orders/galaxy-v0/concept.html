    Three things only you can settle

    • Dotted continents, or solid land?Dots keep the globe light and readable at phone size. Solid land looks more like a toy planet.
    • How much should a dome show?Here a place is five to ten blocks and one landmark: PARC's terraces, CERN's ring, Helsinki's green dome, the Golden Gate. More detail costs file size, and the game stays one file.
    • History's order, or the course's order?This line runs by year, so California comes back in 2013. Following the course instead gives a tidier line and a less honest timeline.

    Neighbours in one region share a planet. Their domes are fanned out so each stays readable, and the light marks the true spot. Dates and places here are the well known ones; in the product every one carries a source link, as ADR 0010 requires. The plan is in docs/GALAXY.md.
