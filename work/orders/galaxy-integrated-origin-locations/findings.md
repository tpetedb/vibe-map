# Source audit

Issue 168 tracks origin source quality. A 22 September 2026 follow-up audit found ten already-integrated topic origins whose source established a dated event, publication or registration but did not establish the city assigned to it.

## Opened primary sources and fixes

- `ci`: [GitHub's 8 August 2019 Actions announcement](https://github.blog/news-insights/product-news/github-actions-now-supports-ci-cd/) establishes CI/CD, YAML workflows and workflows kept as repository code. It does not locate the announcement in San Francisco. The dated online announcement moves to `the-internet`; the Jenkins echo is omitted because a topic cannot stand twice at one place.
- `dotfiles`: [GitHub's 10 April 2008 launch post](https://github.blog/2008-04-10-we-launched/) says GitHub is officially live. Its only San Francisco text is a current promotion unrelated to the 2008 post. The launch moves to `the-internet`.
- `github`: the same [10 April 2008 launch post](https://github.blog/2008-04-10-we-launched/) supports the launch date and wording, not a launch city. The launch moves to `the-internet`; the separately sourced Microsoft acquisition remains in Redmond.
- `languages`: [the Go FAQ](https://go.dev/doc/faq) names the designers, Google and the 10 November 2009 public open source release. It does not mention Mountain View. The release moves to `the-internet`.
- `tests`: [Anthropic's 4 October 2023 evaluation article](https://www.anthropic.com/news/evaluating-ai-systems) is a dated online publication with no San Francisco location. The optional echo is omitted because the topic already has an online primary; the article remains a topic source.
- `interfaces`: [Anthropic's 25 November 2024 MCP announcement](https://www.anthropic.com/news/model-context-protocol) establishes the dated publication and the standard's purpose, with no San Francisco location. The announcement moves to `the-internet`.
- `unix`: [The Open Group registry entry](https://www.opengroup.org/openbrand/register/brand3725.htm) identifies Apple, macOS 26.0 Tahoe, UNIX 03 and the 29 August 2025 registration date, with no Cupertino location. The registration moves to `a-standards-body`.
- `yaml`: [GitHub's 8 August 2019 Actions announcement](https://github.blog/news-insights/product-news/github-actions-now-supports-ci-cd/) establishes the YAML workflow syntax online, with no San Francisco location. The optional echo is omitted because the topic already has an online primary; the Actions history remains covered by its existing source.
- `zsh`: [Apple's 7 October 2019 Catalina availability announcement](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2019/10/macos-catalina-is-available-today/) establishes a worldwide software availability milestone, with no Cupertino event location. The announcement moves to `the-internet`.
- `config`: [the October 2013 first edition of ECMA-404](https://ecma-international.org/wp-content/uploads/ECMA-404_1st_edition_october_2013.pdf) establishes Ecma International, the JSON data interchange syntax, the first edition and adoption by postal vote in October 2013. It exposes no Geneva imprint. The publication moves to `a-standards-body` and cites the dated first edition directly.

No source was used to turn an employer, present headquarters, footer or private development context into a historical event location.
