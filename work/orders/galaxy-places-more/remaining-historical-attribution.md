## Relevant primary evidence for targeted source strengthening

These links were also opened while deciding which rows belong above. They can repair provenance notes without inventing new city claims:

- [Ecma's actual June 1997 first edition of ECMA-262](https://ecma-international.org/wp-content/uploads/ECMA-262_1st_edition_june_1997.pdf) prints Geneva in its historical publication imprint. This supports the existing `web` ECMAScript publication origin more directly than today's footer. The equivalent [October 2013 ECMA-404 PDF](https://ecma-international.org/wp-content/uploads/ECMA-404_1st_edition_october_2013.pdf) was opened but did not expose a Geneva imprint in the text returned, so `config` remains listed above pending direct imprint evidence or an abstract place.
- [Python's original copyright notice](https://www.python.org/doc/copyright/) identifies Stichting Mathematisch Centrum in Amsterdam for 1991-1995 and CWI as the initial source of the software. This is historical evidence alongside Guido's timeline, rather than a current-office inference.
- [Bell Labs' own Unix history](https://www.nokia.com/bell-labs/unix-history/chaos.html) explicitly identifies the small 1969 research group in Murray Hill. It is a stronger primary geographical source than the abbreviated ETHW Unix article.
- [The original Seventh Edition manual](https://9p.io/7thEdMan/v7vol1.pdf) prints January 1979 and Bell Telephone Laboratories, Murray Hill on its title page. That supports the archive's publication-site association for the `readme` topic as well as `headless`.

No quick search produced primary evidence for the remaining specific event-city claims above. That is a bounded evidence gap, not proof that a city is historically wrong. A claim tied to a dated author's university address is also narrower than locating a whole conference or every contributor there. Preserve that distinction in any wording changes.
